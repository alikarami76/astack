Use flynn's AI

flynn's AI runs on your own computer, with your own Claude account. This one command installs the flynn agent, and Claude Code too if you don't have it yet.

curl -fsSL https://your-server/flynn/agent/install.sh | bash

Paste it into Terminal. Then sign in and open Connect flynn to link the agent to your account.

Everything a design team needs, in one place

  • Designs on the canvas

    Draw, lay out and prototype on a fast canvas. Ask flynn to build a screen, tidy a layout or try another direction, and watch it happen.

  • Documents

    Write letters, release notes and reports as pages, laid out with the same design system as your screens, then export them.

  • Learned design systems

    flynn reads your existing designs and learns your colors, type, spacing and components, then follows them and flags what breaks them.

  • Organizations

    Group teams under one organization. Shared fonts and design system libraries reach every team, and owners can manage access in one place.
